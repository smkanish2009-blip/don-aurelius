## 🎬 Production & Audio-Visual Notes

### 1. Visual Aesthetics (Apple HIG & MotionSites Standard)
- **Color Palette:** Deep Obsidian (`#030508`), Imperial Gold (`#F5D061`), Cyan Radar (`#38BDF8`), Emerald Live (`#10B981`).
- **Typography Overlays:** All on-screen titles, step badges, and terminal readouts must use **SF Pro Display** or **JetBrains Mono** with `-0.025em` tracking.
- **Micro-Animations:** Use physical spring curves on zoom-ins; avoid static linear transitions.

### 2. Audio Design & SFX Cue Sheet
- **0:00:** Sub-bass whoosh + fast cinematic ticking clock begins.
- **0:12:** Tactile mechanical keystroke SFX when `.env` is renamed.
- **0:18:** Satisfying metallic switch click as **Algo Trading** turns green.
- **0:28:** Digital pulse / data stream chirp as MT5 terminal connection handshake completes.
- **0:38:** Deep mechanical vault locking sound when the **Demo Guardrail** badge locks on screen.
- **0:52:** Power-up synth riser when `Start_Don_Aurelius.bat` executes.
- **0:59:** Sub-bass impact braam fading immediately to dead silence on the final logo card.

### 3. Speech Metrics
- **Total Word Count:** 142 words
- **Target Duration:** 60.0 seconds
- **Average Reading Rate:** 142 wpm (~2.36 words per second) — optimal for high engagement and zero listener fatigue.
