Because our products consist of immediately executable compiled digital code, proprietary analytical models, and personalized API authentication credentials, they are deemed digital goods that are rendered accessible immediately upon delivery.

EU Statutory Consumer Notice (Directive 2011/83/EU Article 16(m)): Under European Union and United Kingdom consumer protection laws, the standard 14-day statutory right of withdrawal does not apply to the supply of digital content not supplied on a tangible medium once the performance has begun with the consumer’s prior express consent and acknowledgment that they thereby lose their right of withdrawal. By requesting, generating, or downloading an active software license key or terminal binary, you explicitly consent to immediate fulfillment and acknowledge that the statutory right of withdrawal is waived upon delivery.

2. Standard Refund Conditions

To ensure fairness and prevent unauthorized algorithmic duplication, the following refund standards apply:

  • Delivered & Activated License Keys: Once a digital terminal license key, compiled EA file, or API access secret is issued and activated on your MetaTrader 5 terminal, all sales are final and non-refundable.

  • Verified Technical Defects: In the highly improbable event that a verified technical defect in the Don Aurelius core binaries renders the software inoperable on supported Windows 11/x86_64 MetaTrader 5 environments, and our quantitative engineering team is unable to resolve the defect within fourteen (14) business days of written notice, a full refund of any licensing fees paid will be granted.

3. Non-Eligibility for Market Execution Outcomes

Trading losses, broker slippage, or unfavorable market price fluctuations do NOT constitute grounds for a refund. As emphasized in our Terms and Conditions and mandatory CFTC Rule 4.41 disclosures, algorithmic quantitative models operate in probabilistic financial markets where risk of capital loss is an inherent reality. We make no representations or guarantees of profits, and software performance cannot be refunded on the basis of market outcomes.
