Governed by: European ePrivacy Directive & GDPR Art. 5(3) • Founder: SM.KANISH • Updated: September 29, 2026

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text data files placed upon your computer or mobile device when accessing web applications. HTML5 Local Storage (localStorage) is a modern, high-performance browser technology that allows applications to store client-side preferences without transmitting unnecessary data back to web servers on every HTTP request.

Don Aurelius operates with an ultra-strict Zero-Surveillance Architecture. We do NOT drop third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, behavioural profiling beacons, or canvas fingerprinting scripts.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Embedded Content

This website utilizes static content delivery networks (CDNs) for open-source mathematical and graphics libraries:

  • cdnjs / Cloudflare: Serves the Three.js 3D WebGL library. Cloudflare may log basic network metadata to defend against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) threats.
  • Google Fonts: Delivers typography stylesheets. Google does not set tracking cookies when serving fonts.

4. How to Manage, Revoke, or Delete Cookies

You have full sovereign control over your browser’s storage. You can revoke consent, clear existing storage keys, or block cookies entirely through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies → Clear browsing data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

If you choose to delete or reject da_cookie_consent, the privacy banner will simply re-render upon your next visit.
