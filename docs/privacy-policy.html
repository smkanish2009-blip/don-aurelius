Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • Governing Controller: Don Aurelius Quantitative Technologies • Founder: SM.KANISH

1. Sovereign Commitment to Data Privacy

Don Aurelius Sovereign Syndicate (“Don Aurelius”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), under the strategic architectural direction of founder SM.KANISH, is dedicated to protecting your fundamental digital right to privacy. We strictly observe international privacy legislation, including the European Union and United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA / CPRA), and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

Our core engineering philosophy enforces Strict Data Minimization: We only collect data that is technically essential to operate, secure, and verify access to our quantitative algorithmic platform.

2. Data We Collect & Lawful Grounds

Under GDPR Article 6, we process data solely under the lawful bases of Legitimate Interests (maintaining platform security, preventing denial-of-service cyberattacks) and Explicit Consent (when you voluntarily submit contact or verification inquiries).

A. Technical & Operational Telemetry (Automatically Logged)

  • Network Identifiers: Obfuscated IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and timestamp of request (processed exclusively for distributed DDoS mitigation and server log security).
  • Local Preferences: Storage of your explicit cookie banner consent preference (stored strictly locally within your browser via localStorage.getItem('da_cookie_consent')).

B. Voluntary Verification & Inquiry Information

If you choose to submit a verification dossier request or contact our trading desk, you may provide:

  • Your Full Name
  • Corporate or Personal Email Address
  • Telegram Handle (used exclusively for authorized bot terminal whitelisting)
  • Trading Entity Classification (e.g., Independent Quantitative Researcher, Prop Firm)
Absolute Financial Privacy Guarantee: Don Aurelius does NEVER collect, store, transmit, or have access to your personal brokerage account passwords, MT5 investor master passwords, private wallet cryptographic keys, or banking account credentials. Quantitative terminal execution happens locally or through your privately configured MetaTrader 5 broker API endpoints.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Global Data Transfers

To serve this web dossier with ultra-low latency and sovereign security, we utilize trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

  • GitHub Pages (Microsoft Corporation): Global content hosting and static web distribution. Subject to standard EU Model Contractual Clauses.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): Typeface stylesheets cached locally and rendered with privacy-preserving IP anonymization.
  • Telegram Open Platform (Telegram FZ-LLC): For bidirectional messaging telemetry if you interact directly with @DonAurelius_AI_bot.

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads Remarketing). We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to any third party.

4. Data Retention Protocols

We maintain strict data expiration lifecycles:

  • Security Server Logs: Rotated and permanently purged every 30 days.
  • Verification Inquiries: Stored in an encrypted offline administrative archive for up to 12 months for compliance audit trails, after which they are irreversibly destroyed unless ongoing client engagement exists.

5. Your Statutory Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Laws)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, Don Aurelius extends comprehensive sovereign data rights to all users:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a machine-readable copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request immediate correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You may request the immediate, permanent deletion of your inquiry and contact records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You have the right to withdraw previously granted consent at any time without penalty.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, alter software functionality, or charge differential pricing based upon the exercise of your statutory privacy rights.

6. Security Architecture

Our infrastructure enforces Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) cryptographic transit encryption for all HTTP web requests. All internal administrative verification queues are guarded with multi-factor authentication and hardware key verification overseen directly by strategic leadership.
